STS is a tech support company aiming to help older adults with their technical problems. In this website, you can create a support ticket through the form on the "Create Request" page. Once the request is submitted, it is sent to a database where it awaits a technician. The technician will read the request to understand the issue and will reach out to you either through email or a phone call, depending on urgency.
